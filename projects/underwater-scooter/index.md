### Trying fiberglass reinforcement

The watertightness I could achieve with 3D printing was limited, so I tried reinforcing the printed parts with fiberglass. This was my first time working with the material.

The reinforcement made the structure much stronger, but it also made the build more time-consuming. The fiberglass I used was better suited to larger boat surfaces than to a small design with many corners and fine details.
